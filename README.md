## ✦ Key Systems & Architectural Highlights

### 1. 🪐 Pinned Kinetic Hero Canvas (300 Frames)
- **Engine:** Scroll-linked HTML5 `<canvas>` sequence driven by 300 chronological frames.
- **Performance:** RAF coalesced draw scheduling with `alpha: false`, high-DPI scaling capped at 2.0 DPR, instant Frame 1 rendering, and mobile stride optimization.
- **Kinetic Typography:** Two ambient counter-drifting flowing text marquee layers providing layered cinematic depth behind monumental Syne typography.

### 2. 🔍 `ScrollExpandMedia` Expanding Stage (241 Frames)
- **Engine:** Pinned 240vh expanding media container inspired by the ECHO kinetic canvas architecture.
- **Motion:** Dynamically expands from a compact 320×420 card to full viewport bounds (`94vw` / `86vh`), scrubbing through a 241-frame sequence in real time.
- **Perimeter Layout:** Monolithic perimeter content reveal (header at top, 3 architectural pillars and summary at bottom) leaving the center artwork 100% open and unobstructed.

### 3. 🧭 "Where I've Been" Horizontal Spatial Canvas
- **Engine:** Sticky horizontal timeline translating vertical user scroll into continuous horizontal camera progression (`translate3d`).
- **Depth:** Oversized background year typography (`2026`, `OCT 2026`) with parallax drift and ambient counter-drifting background tickers.
- **Milestones:** Dedicated chapters for SYNK AI Research & Machine Learning Engineering and Boston Consulting Group (BCG) Data Science Simulation.

### 4. 🎡 Flagship Systems & 3D Drum Reel
- **Featured Flagship (Lumine AI):** Interactive liquid perspective stage with diagnostic telemetry and live tab inspection (Neural Architecture, Edge Latency, Privacy Guarantee).
- **Specialized Systems Archive:** Mathematical 3D perspective drum showcase with step navigation, direct modal dossier inspection, and live deployment links.
